Privacy Policy & User Rights

Last Updated: March 2, 2026

This archive operates on a foundation of digital minimalism and radical respect for your autonomy. I do not want your data, and I have architected this site to avoid collecting it whenever technically possible.

1. Age Restriction & COPPA Compliance

This website is strictly for individuals who are 18 years of age or older.

By accessing this site, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old. I do not knowingly collect, request, or process personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

In strict compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international equivalents, if I discover that any communication or data has been submitted by a minor under the age of 13 (or under 18 per site policy), that information will be permanently and immediately erased from my servers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has provided me with personal information via direct email, please contact me immediately so it can be destroyed.

2. The Philosophy of Zero Data (No Cookies)

This website runs without client-side JavaScript. Because of this architectural choice, the invasive tracking mechanisms standard on the modern web simply cannot function here.

  • No Cookies: This site does not set, use, or read cookies. There is no cookie consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • No Cross-Site Tracking: There are no advertising pixels, social media tracking scripts, or fingerprinting technologies embedded in this code.

3. Web Analytics (GoatCounter)

To measure the reach of my essays and projects without violating your privacy, I use GoatCounter. GoatCounter is an open-source, privacy-first web analytics platform.

GoatCounter is designed to be fully compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) without requiring a consent notice. It works by:

  • Discarding your IP address and generating a non-reversible, anonymous hash.
  • Tracking only generic data: the page visited, the referring domain, and standard user-agent strings (e.g., browser type).
  • Storing no personal data and creating no persistent tracking profiles.

4. Information You Voluntarily Provide

The only personal data I process is data you explicitly and voluntarily send to me.

  • Direct Email: If you email me, I will process your email address and the contents of your message solely for the purpose of replying to your inquiry.
  • Value for Value (Support): If you support this archive via crypto wallets (BTC/ETH) or local fiat wallets (GCash/Maya), those transactions are governed by their respective blockchain networks or banking institutions. I do not collect or store your financial identities on this web server.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you reside within the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess specific rights regarding your personal data (such as an email you sent me) under the GDPR. You have the right to:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data I hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request that I permanently delete our email correspondence.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that I temporarily halt processing your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please send an email to my direct contact address listed on the About page. I will comply within 30 days, free of charge.

6. Data Retention & Server Logs

The hosting provider for this website temporarily logs standard connection data (such as time of access, requested URL, and generic user-agent strings) to ensure network security, debug server errors, and prevent DDoS attacks. This is a standard infrastructure requirement. These raw logs are routinely purged and are never combined with other data to identify individual users.

7. External Links

My archive frequently links to external websites. While I link to high-quality sources, I do not control their privacy architectures. Once you click a link and leave this site, my privacy policy no longer applies. You are subject to the policies (and potential tracking) of that external site.

8. Changes to this Policy

If the underlying architecture of this site changes in a way that affects privacy, I will update this page and change the "Last Updated" date at the top. However, my commitment to a static, JS-free, privacy-first web will not change.
