Zero Tracking

Cookie Policy (Or the Lack Thereof)

Last Updated: March 2, 2026

The short version: This website does not use cookies. You will never be interrupted by a "Cookie Consent" banner here, because there is absolutely nothing you need to consent to.

1. What Are Cookies?

In web terms, a cookie is a small text file saved on your computer or mobile device by a website you visit. Originally, they were designed for legitimate functionality—like keeping you logged into an account or remembering the contents of a shopping cart.

However, the modern web has weaponized cookies. They are primarily used by third-party advertising networks to track your behavior across multiple domains, build a psychological profile of your habits, and serve you hyper-targeted advertisements. I reject this model entirely.

2. Why I Do Not Use Them

This archive is a purely static, structural entity. It relies strictly on HTML and CSS. There are no user accounts to log into, no comments to remember via local storage, and no shopping carts. Therefore, strictly necessary (functional) cookies are not required.

Furthermore, because I value digital minimalism and human privacy, I refuse to implement non-essential cookies.

  • No Advertising Cookies: I do not run ads.
  • No Social Media Pixels: I do not embed Facebook, X/Twitter, or LinkedIn tracking scripts.
  • No Third-Party Media Trackers: Instead of embedding tracking-heavy YouTube or Spotify players, I host my media locally using standard HTML5 <audio> and <video> tags.

3. How I Handle Analytics Without Cookies

As detailed in the Privacy Policy, I use GoatCounter to understand my site traffic. GoatCounter is a privacy-first analytics tool that explicitly does not use cookies.

It generates a temporary, anonymous hash based on your IP address and user-agent. This hash cannot be used to identify you, cannot track you across other websites, and cannot be tied to a persistent personal profile. It simply allows me to count how many times a specific essay or photo gallery was viewed.

4. External Links and Third-Party Sites

While my site remains a cookie-free zone, I frequently link out to external resources, articles, and references. If you click a link and leave this archive, you are entering a different jurisdiction.

Those external websites will likely deploy cookies according to their own policies. I have no control over the tracking infrastructure of the rest of the internet. I encourage you to use strict browser settings (such as Firefox's Enhanced Tracking Protection or a privacy-focused browser like Brave) to protect yourself when navigating away from this site.

5. How to Manage Cookies Universally

Even though you do not need to manage cookies for this specific website, you have the absolute right to control how your browser handles them globally. You can delete all cookies that are already on your device and set most modern browsers to prevent them from being placed.

For more information on managing your global footprint, you can visit aboutcookies.org.
